Gift Record Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 (revised October 30, 2026)

This is an English translation of the Korean original. In case of any discrepancy, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information We Collect

The app itself does not directly collect any personal information such as your name, contact details, or location. However, the Google AdMob SDK collects the information described in Section 5 to serve ads.

2. How Data Is Stored

Gift records you enter (names, dates, prices, notes, photos, etc.) are stored only in your device's internal storage and are not sent to any external server. No sign-up or login is required.

3. Sharing with Third Parties

This app displays ads through Google AdMob. Information collected by the AdMob SDK may be shared with Google for ad serving and fraud prevention. See Section 5 for details.

4. External Links

The "Recommendations" tab contains affiliate links to external shopping sites. Tapping a link opens the site in an external browser; purchases, payments, and personal data handling on external sites are governed by each site's own policy. Recommendation product information is provided by a file published by the developer that the app loads; no personal information is transmitted in this process.

5. Advertising

This app serves ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, the AdMob SDK may collect the following information.

  • Device or other IDs (advertising ID)
  • IP address (to estimate approximate location)
  • Ad interaction records (such as ad clicks)
  • Diagnostic information (such as crash logs)

Collected information may be shared with Google for ad serving and fraud prevention. If you do not want personalized ads, turn off "Ads personalization" in your device settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
